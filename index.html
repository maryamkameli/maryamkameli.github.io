I work at the intersection of generative 3D representation and human visual perception. My research asks what a rendering system should actually optimize for when a person, not a metric, is the one looking.

Right now I build photorealistic human avatars using 3D Gaussian Splatting and diffusion models, and I design controlled studies to measure how rendering choices change what people perceive. I am especially interested in perceptual accessibility: how varying levels of visual fidelity affect viewers with sensory deficits, and what that means for the systems we build.

Before my PhD I worked on assistive robotics, biomedical signal processing, and deep learning for seizure prediction. I hold an MS in Computer Science from the University of Minnesota Duluth and a BS in Electrical Engineering from K. N. Toosi University of Technology in Tehran.

Research

2026 – present · University of Minnesota

Photorealistic Avatar Synthesis with 3D Gaussian Splatting

A human avatar pipeline built on 3D Gaussian Splatting and diffusion-based generative models, targeting real-time rendering at photorealistic fidelity. The pipeline exposes controllable fidelity levels, so one identity can be rendered along a photorealism gradient and evaluated for perceptual accessibility under sensory deficits.

3D Gaussian SplattingDiffusion modelsPerception

2024 – 2026 · University of Minnesota

Locomotion and Rendering Effects on Spatial Memory and Cybersickness

Sketch-like non-photorealistic continuous transitions for VR locomotion that improve spatial awareness while reducing cybersickness. Built and deployed controlled remote experiments end to end, from stimulus generation through analysis of ordinal, non-normal response data.

VR locomotionNPRUser studies

2022 – 2023 · University of Minnesota Duluth

Autonomous Mapping and Navigation for Social Robots

LiDAR and NVIDIA Jetson Nano integrated with a Pepper social robot over ROS, offloading perception and mapping to improve real-time reliability in human-occupied scenes. Deployed across nine nursing homes in Minnesota with a web app for live facility mapping and a staff dashboard for resident activity sessions.

ROSSLAMAssistive robotics

2026 · Hennepin Healthcare Research Institute

High-Density EMG for Spinal Cord Stimulation

An EMG recording system mapping epidural spinal cord stimulation patterns to lower-limb muscle activation for a motor-complete spinal cord injury restoration study, including flexible electrode-array PCBs and a normalized data pipeline for Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization of stimulation preferences across a 12-month trial.

EMGBayesian optimizationHardware

2022 – 2023 · University of Minnesota Duluth

Real-Time Human Fall Detection

A real-time IoT backend streaming smartwatch time-series data to the cloud, with a Python feature-engineering and classification pipeline separating falls from routine activity, containerized with Docker and served on Azure Kubernetes Service.

Time seriesKubernetesIoT

2020 – 2021 · K. N. Toosi University of Technology

Seizure Prediction with Deep Learning

CNN, LSTM, and Bi-LSTM models over EEG signals that predict epileptic seizures up to one hour before onset, turning raw recordings into clinically actionable early warnings. Undergraduate thesis.

Selected Projects

Markerless Human Motion Capture and Animation

Computer Graphics · Fall 2025

End-to-end video-to-animation pipeline using ViTPose for markerless pose estimation, Segment Anything for 3D mesh extraction, and OpenGL for rendering, with Savitzky-Golay filtering and spline interpolation smoothing noisy pose trajectories into realistic motion.

Video-Based 3D Reconstruction and Segmentation

Robot Vision · Fall 2023

A pipeline reconstructing accurate 3D models from video, combining SAM with monocular depth to improve segmentation under occlusion and transparency.

Neural Machine Translation and LLM Fine-Tuning

Advanced NLP · Spring 2022

An attention-based transformer NMT system in PyTorch using transfer learning to lift quality for low-resource languages, plus BERT and GPT-2 fine-tuned on SuperGLUE to compare language understanding and text representation.
